# Example: investor pitch (concept only; no fabricated traction)

**Audience:** seed investors. **Style:** `startup-narrative` with restrained `swiss-grid` discipline. **Primary goal:** investor understands problem/solution and can assess a real ask.

Story map: 1) compelling observed user pain (source needed), 2) cost of status quo, 3) single-sentence solution, 4) authentic product flow screenshot, 5) addressable market methodology (not invented TAM), 6) why this implementation differs, 7) real business model assumptions, 8) legitimate pilot evidence (or explicitly planned pilot), 9) GTM experiment, 10) risks, 11) team credentials (verified), 12) precise funding ask with use of funds (user supplied).

Visual treatment: large proof objects, restrained typography, true product screenshot over conceptual 3D render, consistently sourced charts. Metrics have N/timeframe. If no real numbers exist, show methodology and sensitivity model, not speculative certainty.
