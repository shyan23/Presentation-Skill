# Source integrity and provenance

## Claim classes
- **Given**: user-provided text, measurements, or files; preserve exactly unless clearly revised.
- **Verified**: supported by an accessible document, data table, paper, or other primary source; attach reference and extraction date.
- **Derived**: calculation from verified/given numbers; record formula and units.
- **Assumption**: design assumptions, forecast, interpolation; label it visibly if material.
- **Placeholder**: content not ready, marked `[SOURCE NEEDED]`, `[DATA NEEDED]`, or `[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER]`.
- **Concept**: artistic visual metaphor; never imply the picture documents a real event or product.

## Slide-level evidence ledger
For every nontrivial claim, record slide ID, literal claim, source identifier + page/row/time, confidence, evidence type, and reviewer decision. Prefer direct links and exact page references. Confirm chart titles and units match evidence. Avoid pseudo-citations to unverified AI-generated summaries.

## Never
- Never fabricate dates, statistics, market projections, citations, empirical findings, product screenshots, or precise ratios.
- Never transplant a creator's distinct wording from a video or article without appropriate quoting/attribution.
- Never change a measured number for layout symmetry, and never use an unlabeled axis truncation to exaggerate differences.
- Never present a hypothetical diagram as an observed experiment.
